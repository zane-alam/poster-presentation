Demo routing rule: risk of 0.5 or more, or any confidence under 0.7, goes to the safety lane. Unclear or advice goes to the LLM lane. Everything else is routine.

How these numbers are estimated
  • Assumes 300 input and 100 output tokens per request.
  • Jev: $0.042 to $0.084 per million input tokens (sources differ), output free. That gives about $13 to $25 per million requests.
  • LLM-only baseline: $0.20 per million input tokens (a low-priced LLM) and output at about 5 times the input price. That gives about $160 per million requests. Pricier LLMs cost more.
  • These prices come from unofficial sources and Jev’s speed and price claims are vendor-reported. Check official price pages before using them.
  • Excludes speech-to-text, hosting, banking-system costs and human agent time.
  • At 0% escalation, the saving matches the poster’s decision-step estimate of about 6 to 13 times.
  • A pilot would replace all of this with measured numbers.

03 — Risks and what we recommend

Pilot first, scale on evidence

  • Voice never pays alone

    Fingerprint or PIN approves every payment, with limits, so a fake voice cannot move money.

  • Test Bangla first

    Jev’s accuracy in languages other than English is not published. Test Bangla and mixed speech before launch.

  • Share minimal data

    Jev is a third-party service from a young company. Send only what it needs, masked, and keep a fallback.

  • Low scores go to a human

    A confidence score is not a guarantee. Tune the limits in the pilot and route doubtful requests to an agent.

  1. 1

    Pilot

    Three safe actions with a small group: check balance, pay a saved payee, pay a saved bill.

  2. 2

    Measure

    Cost per request, completion time, error rate and customer satisfaction, against today’s app.

  3. 3

    Scale

    Add advice and more services only if the results and the safety checks hold.
